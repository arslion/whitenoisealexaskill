# Privacy Policy for "White Noise" Alexa Skill

**Last Updated:** 20 November 2025\
**Effective Date:** Immediately

## 1. Introduction

The **White Noise** Alexa skill provides soothing white noise audio for
babies and families.
This Privacy Policy explains what information is (and is not) collected
when you use the skill.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe
experience for all users.

## 2. Information We Collect

The **White Noise** skill **does not collect, store, or process any
personal information**.

Specifically, the skill does **not**:

-   Collect your name, email, address, or contact details
-   Store voice recordings
-   Create or store user accounts
-   Track usage or behaviour
-   Collect device identifiers
-   Use cookies or analytics

Any voice commands you speak (e.g., "Alexa, open White Noise", "Alexa,
stop") are processed **solely by Amazon Alexa**, according to Amazon's
own privacy practices. We do **not** have access to your voice
recordings or transcripts.

## 3. How Your Information Is Used

Since we do not collect any personal information, the skill uses **no
data** for any processing other than responding to your commands.

Your requests are handled entirely by the Amazon Alexa service to:

-   Start playback of white noise
-   Stop playback
-   Respond to help or fallback intents

We receive **no data** from these interactions.

## 4. Information Sharing

We do **not** share any data with third parties because we do not
collect any data in the first place.

## 5. Children's Privacy

This skill may be used by parents to help soothe infants; however:

-   The skill **does not collect any personal information from children
    or adults**.\
-   The skill complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act
    (COPPA) because it **collects no data**.

If you believe that any inappropriate data has been collected through
Amazon's systems, please contact Amazon directly.

## 6. Security

The skill does not store any data.
All interactions take place within the secure Amazon Alexa ecosystem.
We do not operate any external servers that process or store
information.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on the website hosting this policy.

Your continued use of the skill after updates means you accept the
revised policy.
